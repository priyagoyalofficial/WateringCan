    Why plants die, and how this helps
    The date tells you when to check, not when to pour

    Do the finger test first. Push a finger into the soil up to the depth the card shows. Still damp? Tap Still damp and the app checks back in a couple of days. Most overwatering happens when people water on a fixed schedule.

    How much to pour

    Each card shows an amount worked out from the pot width, about a quarter of the pot's volume. A 6-inch pot takes about 1¾ cups. Measure it a few times with a measuring cup until you can judge it by eye. Always empty the saucer after 15 minutes. A pot left sitting in runoff stays waterlogged no matter how carefully you measured.

    It learns your home

    Each time you report soil that's still damp or bone dry, that plant's schedule shifts. After a few waterings it fits your windows and your pots, not an average house.

    Your chunky mix turning hydrophobic

    Peat and bark repel water once they dry out completely, so the water runs straight down the sides. Soak the pot from the bottom for 20–30 minutes, or water twice, 5 minutes apart. Adding coco coir in place of some of the peat also helps it take up water again.

    Zone 10b seasons

    Hot, dry summers and fall Santa Ana winds dry pots quickly. Plants drink less December through February. The schedule adjusts by month, and fertilizing pauses in winter.
